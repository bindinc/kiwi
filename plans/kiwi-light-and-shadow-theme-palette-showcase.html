Semantic components

Pantone names belong in the source layer. KIWI components consume roles such as action, information, attention, muted, success, warning, and danger.

Actions and accents

Static color specimens. Blue Fusion identifies primary actions; secondary actions use an outline. These samples are not controls.

Operational states stay semantic

The theme does not force safety-critical or call-state colors into the decorative palette.

Recommended theme direction

Use the palette to create hierarchy, not to replace every color. Accessibility neutrals and operational state colors remain first-class semantic tools.

  • Cloud Dancer page canvas
  • White work surfaces; elevation only for overlays
  • Blue Fusion navigation and action
  • Veiled Vista calm selection
  • Dark ink on mid-tone accents
  • Semantic safety colors preserved
Planning artifact only. Pantone colors shown on screen are sRGB approximations and do not replace physical Pantone standards.
